Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

What we collect

When you ask for a free case review we collect what you type into the form: your name, phone number, email if you give one, and your answers about the accident (when it happened, whether you were hurt, whose fault it was, and whether you already have a lawyer). We also record that you agreed to be contacted. Like most websites, basic technical data such as your browser type may be logged by our hosting provider.

How we use it

We use your information to have a participating local injury law firm review your request and contact you. We may also contact you ourselves about your request.

Who we share it with

We share your information with a participating local injury law firm and with the service providers that run this website and our forms (for example our hosting, form and texting tools). We do not sell your information. We may share information if the law requires it.

Calls and texts

If you check the consent box, Hampton Roads Accident Help and a participating local injury law firm may call, text (including automated texts) or email you about your request. Consent is not required to get help. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. Message and data rates may apply.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed for your request and our records, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, by writing to info@everlastmarketing.com.

Children

This site is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy at any time. The date at the top shows the latest version.

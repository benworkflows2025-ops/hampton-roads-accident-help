What to do after a car accident in Portsmouth

Short answer

Stop, check for injuries and call 911 if anyone is hurt. Swap names, license and insurance info with the other driver, but don't admit fault or talk about who caused it. Get checked by a doctor right away, then get your Portsmouth police crash report. Don't talk to the other driver's insurance company.

Right after the crash, in this order

  1. Stop and call 911 if anyone is hurt. Virginia law says police must be told right away when someone is injured or killed. Even if it seems minor, call the police so there is a report.
  2. Give and get information. Your name, address, driver's license number and vehicle registration. Help anyone who is hurt get medical care.
  3. Take photos of the cars, the road and any injuries, and get the names and numbers of witnesses.
  4. See a doctor right away, even if you feel fine. Some injuries show up days later, and a gap between the crash and your first visit is something insurance companies use against you.
  5. Get the crash report (details below).

Where this comes from: Va. Code § 46.2-371, Va. Code § 46.2-894

What not to do

  • Don't admit fault or apologize at the scene, to the other driver, or to anyone else. Stick to the facts.
  • Don't talk to the other driver's insurance company. Don't give them a recorded statement.
  • Don't sign anything or accept a quick offer. Once you sign a release, you usually can't ask for more.
  • Don't post about the crash or your injuries online. Insurance companies look.
  • Don't skip or stop medical care. Follow your doctor's plan, and keep every bill, receipt and note about missed work.

Getting your crash report in Portsmouth

  • Portsmouth Police Records Unit
  • 700 Crawford St., Portsmouth, VA 23704
  • 757-393-8737
  • Call ahead to confirm the fee.

Where this comes from: source

If the crash was on the interstate (I-64, I-264, I-664 and others), Virginia State Police usually handle it. Their reports come from the Virginia DMV: use form CRD 93, $8 (refunded if no report is found), by mail, fax or at any DMV office.

If a city vehicle hit you

If the other vehicle belonged to the City of Portsmouth (a city truck or bus, for example), Virginia law says you generally must give the city written notice of your claim within 6 months. That is much shorter than the usual 2 years.

Where this comes from: Va. Code § 15.2-209

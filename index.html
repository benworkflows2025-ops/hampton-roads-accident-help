Why now

In Virginia you usually have 2 years to file an injury claim. The other driver's insurance may call you fast. Don't give them a statement or sign anything until you've had a free case review.

Where this comes from: Va. Code § 8.01-243

Answers people look for after a crash

  • Do I have to call the police after an accident in Virginia?

    Yes, if anyone is hurt or killed: Virginia law says police must be told right away.

    Read the answer

  • How long do I have to file a claim in Virginia?

    Usually 2 years from the accident for an injury, and 5 years for damage to your car.

    Read the answer

  • What if the accident was partly my fault?

    Virginia is one of the few places with a strict rule: if you were even a little at fault, you generally can't recover money from the other driver.

    Read the answer

  • Should I talk to the other driver's insurance company?

    No. The other driver's insurance company works for them, not you. Don't give a recorded statement or sign anything.

    Read the answer

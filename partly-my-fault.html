What if the accident was partly my fault?

Short answer

Virginia is one of the few places with a strict rule: if you were even a little at fault, you generally can't recover money from the other driver. That's why who was at fault matters so much here. Never admit fault, even partly, to anyone.

The rule in plain words

Most states lower your payout if you were partly at fault. Virginia doesn't. Under its "contributory negligence" rule, being even slightly at fault can stop you from recovering at all. Only a handful of places still use this rule: Virginia, Alabama, Maryland, North Carolina and Washington, D.C.

Where this comes from: Virginia Lawyer (Virginia State Bar), April 2026

There are exceptions

One is called "last clear chance": if the other driver had the last clear chance to avoid the crash, you may still be able to recover. Whether an exception fits your case is something an attorney has to look at.

Where this comes from: Coutlakis v. CSX Transportation, 293 Va. 212 (2017)

What this means for you

Don't guess about fault, don't apologize, and don't talk to the other driver's insurance company. The other side will look for anything that puts some of the blame on you, even a small comment or a social media post. A free case review can tell you where you stand.
